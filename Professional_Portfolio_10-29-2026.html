Building quantitative assays from concept through validation, with a focus on specificity, sensitivity and reproducibility.

  • Validated FAST™ biosensor performance in single-blinded benchmarking with a collaborator: 71–100% concordance with ELISA, depending on application
  • Developed sandwich ELISAs from scratch for lysozyme and TNF: antibody pairing, coating and blocking optimization, calibration curves, dilution linearity
  • Design and run multiplexed GFET biosensor experiments under BSL-2 containment to characterize specificity, sensitivity and reliability
  • Optimize EDC/NHS aptamer and antifouling PEG surface chemistry for pesticide, drug-of-abuse and protein-toxin (bacterial toxins, ricin) sensors; validated antifouling in soil and urine

Molecular, cellular and tissue assays across in vitro, ex vivo and in vivo systems, from graduate work on enteric viruses to undergraduate research on the gut-brain axis.

  • Mapped gene expression in tissue with RNAscope in situ hybridization, immunofluorescence and confocal microscopy

Turning biosensor chip fabrication into a reliable, scalable process, and checking every chip before it's used.

  • Developed chip fabrication methods, including graphene transfer onto polymer substrates and spin-coating PMMA support films onto graphene-on-copper
  • Built three in-house graphene transfer methods (thermal release tape, electrochemical delamination, chemical etching), ending reliance on outside transfer vendors
